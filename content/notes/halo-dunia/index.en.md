Welcome to **fauzan.id**. This is the first post on this blog.

I'll be writing notes about technology, web development, and other things I'm learning. Most of them will be practical: problems I ran into, how I solved them, and what I learned along the way.

## Why write?

Writing helps me think more clearly. And notes that are shared might help someone else who is stuck on the same problem.

## About this blog

This blog is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and hosted on GitHub Pages, with no server of my own. It is deliberately black and white and simple, so the focus stays on the writing. There's a dark mode too; try the round button in the top right corner.

See you in the next post.
